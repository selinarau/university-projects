# Information Systems and Society (Autumn 2023)

The course of **Information Systems and Society** was organized by **Tampere University** and it included writing three essays about topics related to the course and using given sources. The essays are written in Finnish.

The titles of the essays I have written are:

* Development trends and sub-areas of digitalization and their impacts on Finland (Digitalisaation kehitystrendejä sekä osa-alueitaja niiden vaikutuksia Suomeen)

* Ethical issues in the information society and welfare technology (Tietoyhteiskunnan ja hyvinvointiteknologian eettisiä kysymyksiä)

* The good and bad sides of the internet (Internetin hyviä ja huonoja puolia)
